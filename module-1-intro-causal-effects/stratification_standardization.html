Causal Inference: Stratification & Standardization

Module 1: The Discrete G-Formula in Plain Language

The Core Problem & Solution

Problem: Observational difference E(Y|A=1) - E(Y|A=0) has selection bias because groups differ on confounders X.

Goal: Find counterfactual means E(Y¹) and E(Y⁰): what would happen if everyone were treated versus untreated.

E(Yᵃ) = ∑ₓ E(Y | A=a, X=x) × P(X=x)

ATE = E(Y¹) - E(Y⁰) across the standardized marginal distribution of X.

STEP 1

Stratification (Conditioning)

Group population into slices where confounders X are identical.

  • Within slice X=x, treatment assignment is as good as random (under ignorability).
  • Compute conditional outcome: E(Y | A=a, X=x).
  • Eliminates confounding inside each stratum.
STEP 2

Marginalization (Standardizing)

Re-combine stratum results using the overall population weights.

  • Weight each stratum by P(X=x) (percentage of total population in that stratum).
  • Produces the expected outcome under a hypothetical policy where everyone gets treatment a.

The 4 Necessary Assumptions

  • Ignorability: (Y⁰, Y¹) ⊥ A | X. No unmeasured confounders.
  • Positivity: 0 < P(A=a | X=x) < 1. Both treated and control units exist in every stratum.
  • Consistency: Observed Y equals potential outcome Yᵃ for treatment received.
  • SUTVA: No interference between units. No hidden variations in treatment.

Why It Breaks (Curse of Dimensionality)

  • Works cleanly for 1 or 2 categorical confounders.
  • Fails rapidly with multiple or continuous confounders because strata become empty.
  • Empty cells violate empirical positivity: E(Y | A=a, X=x) cannot be estimated.
  • Next tools in the course (matching, propensity scores, IPTW) solve this scaling problem.

Python Implementation

# Stratification and Standardization in Pandas
# weights: P(X=x)
weights = df['X'].value_counts(normalize=True).to_dict()

# strata_means: E(Y | A=a, X=x)
strata_means = df.groupby(['X', 'A'])['Y'].mean().unstack()

# standardized expected outcomes: sum_x E(Y | A=a, X=x) * P(X=x)
ey1 = sum(strata_means.loc[x, 1] * weights[x] for x in weights)
ey0 = sum(strata_means.loc[x, 0] * weights[x] for x in weights)

ate_standardized = ey1 - ey0
